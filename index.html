KEY FINDING 1

High Lead Time Speculation Risk

Bookings made >180 days in advance exhibit a 55.46% cancellation rate (rising to 67.65% for >365 days).

Long lead times foster speculative bookings where guests reserve options without firm travel commitment.

High inventory holding costs and unfulfilled room allocation blocking short-notice travelers.

Implement automated re-confirmation touchpoints at 90, 60, and 30 days prior to arrival date.

KEY FINDING 2

Non-Refundable Wholesale Deposit Paradox

Non-refundable deposit bookings show an overwhelming 99.36% cancellation rate (14,493 out of 14,586).

Tour operators block room blocks with non-refundable tags far in advance and relinquish unallocated blocks.

Artificial demand signals distorting dynamic pricing models and hotel revenue management.

Restructure wholesale contracts; enforce strict release deadlines and group pre-payment guarantees.
